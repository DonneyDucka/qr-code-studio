Questions, answered.

Are these QR codes free to use commercially?

Yes. The codes you generate are yours to use anywhere - print, web, packaging, signage.

Do the QR codes expire?

No. These are static QR codes that encode your content directly, so they never expire and don't rely on this site staying online.

Will adding a logo break scanning?

Not if it's reasonably sized. We use high error correction, so a centred logo up to ~25% of the code stays reliably scannable. Always test before printing.

What does Pro unlock?

Centre logos, gradients, custom corner styles, vector SVG export, and high-resolution PNG up to 2000px. It's a one-time purchase.
